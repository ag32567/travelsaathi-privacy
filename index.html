Privacy Policy for TravelSaathi

Last updated: September 29, 2026

First of All I would like to thank to you to using TravelSaathi TravelSaathi ("the app") is a travel-planning app that helps you plan trips, estimate budgets, and find flights and hotels. This page explains what information the app handles and how.

No account or sign-in

TravelSaathi does not require you to create an account or sign in. No name, email address, or personal identity is collected or stored by the app.

Smart trip suggestions

Some features of the app, such as understanding trip requests typed in plain language, are powered by AI processing that runs on a backend service operated by the app's developer. When you use these features, the relevant text you enter is sent to that backend, which forwards it to Groq, a third-party AI provider, to generate a response. This is sent only to process your request and is not linked to any personal account, since none exists. The developer's backend does not permanently store this text.

Place lookups and photos

To show information and photos for the destinations you search, the app queries the public Wikipedia API. Only the place name you search is sent; no personal information is included in these requests.

External links

The app includes buttons to search for flights and hotels, which open external websites or apps (such as Google Flights, MakeMyTrip, and Booking.com) in your device's browser or the relevant installed app. Once you leave TravelSaathi, your activity on those sites is governed by their own privacy policies, not this one. TravelSaathi does not receive any data back from these sites.

Maps

Tapping "View on Google Maps" opens Google Maps with a search for the relevant destination. This is handled by the Google Maps app or website on your device, subject to Google's own privacy policy.

Data stored on your device

Your current trip selection, budget, and preferences are kept only in the app's memory while it's running, for your convenience. This data is not transmitted anywhere except as described above (to the AI assistant backend, when you send a chat message).

No advertising or analytics

TravelSaathi does not display ads and does not use analytics or tracking services. If this changes in a future update, this policy will be updated to reflect it before that update is released.

Children's privacy

TravelSaathi is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a revised "Last updated" date.
