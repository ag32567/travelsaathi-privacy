TravelSaathi Privacy Policy

Last updated: Oct 11, 2026

Introduction

Thank you for using TravelSaathi! I'm Aryan Gupta, an IT engineer and I built this app. This policy explains what the app does with your information and who else sees it.

In short:

  • I don't ask you to create an account and the app shows no ads.
  • Trips you plan are saved on your own phone, not on any server of mine.
  • What you type is passed on to an AI service so it can plan your trip. I don't collect or store it myself.
  • The app doesn't ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or payment details.

By using TravelSaathi you agree to this policy. If you don't agree, please don't use the app.

Information the app handles

TravelSaathi handles the following information so it can plan trips for you. I don't collect any of it into a database of my own.

  • What you type is processed by the AI/Model service (Groq) on its servers. I don't control how that service handles, stores or uses the data once it receives it and its own privacy policy applies. Please don't type sensitive personal information such as passwords, ID numbers or health details.
  • You control what you type. Anything you type is passed to an AI/Model service that I don't control, so if you don't want something sent there, don't type it and avoid including personal details in your messages.
  • What you type. Messages in the search bar, questions in the Explore screen and any home city you enter. These are passed on to be processed as described under third-party services below.
  • Trip details. The destination, number of days, budget, number of travellers, expenses you log and trips you choose to save. Saved trips stay on your phone. Basic details such as the destination, days and budget are sent along with what you type.
  • Technical information. Like any app that uses the internet, it reveals your IP address and basic network details to the servers that answer each request.
  • Your first name (optional). If you choose to enter it, it is saved only on your phone to greet you. It is never sent to me or to any service.
  • Trip start date and reminders (optional). If you pick a start date, it is saved on your phone. The app uses it to schedule reminders on your phone and it is sent to Open-Meteo along with the destination to get the weather forecast for your selected dates. The reminders themselves are scheduled on your phone and are never sent anywhere.
  • Stops you tick off. When you mark a stop on a day card as done, that choice is saved only on your phone. It is never sent anywhere.

I don't collect your email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or payment details. The app has no sign-in and I don't use advertising or analytics tools.

If you installed TravelSaathi from Google Play, Google may collect crash and performance data under its own policies.

How I use information

I use your information only to run the app.

  • To plan itineraries and suggest hotels, restaurants and activities.
  • To greet you by your first name, if you chose to enter it.
  • To look up places, photos and weather forecasts for your destination.
  • To remember your trips on your phone between visits.
  • To protect the service from misuse, for example by checking that requests come from the app and limiting how many requests one connection can make.

I don't sell your information. I don't use it for advertising or to build profiles about you.

Third-party services

TravelSaathi sends data to the services below. Each one handles it under its own privacy policy, which I encourage you to read.

  • Cloudflare (my relay server). Receives your message, basic trip details, IP address and request details. It passes requests to the AI service, keeps the AI service key off your phone and keeps a short-term store of generated travel suggestions so repeat requests are faster.
  • Groq (AI Model Provider). Llama Model(Developed by Meta) Receives your message and basic trip details. It writes itineraries, suggestions and answers.
  • Wikipedia and Wikimedia. Receive destination names and image requests. They confirm places and show destination photos.
  • Open-Meteo. Receives the destination name, trip dates and the names of the city you travel from and the destination when distance is measured. It provides weather forecasts.
  • Picsum Photos. Receives an image request and a place identifier. It shows a placeholder photo when no Wikipedia photo is available.

Some buttons and links open other apps or websites, such as Google Search (when you tap a stop on a day card), Google Maps, Google Flights, Booking.com, MakeMyTrip, IRCTC, RedBus, AbhiBus and Zomato. Once you leave TravelSaathi, that site's own policy applies. I don't see what you do there and bookings are made on those sites, not in my app.

Storage, retention and deletion

Your trips are stored on your phone and stay there until you remove them. I do not keep a database of what you type and my relay server may keep short-term technical logs such as request times and error codes to run and protect the service.

To keep the app fast, my relay server also keeps a temporary store of AI-generated travel suggestions, such as hotel ideas, restaurant ideas, cost estimates and day plans for a destination. These are kept for up to 30 days and are filed under place names, trip length and categories. They contain no names, messages or other personal details and they are shared by everyone who plans a trip to the same place.

The services listed above may keep data for their own periods under their own policies.

You can delete your data at any time:

  • Remove a saved trip from the My Trips screen.
  • Change or remove your name in Settings.
  • Clear the app's storage in your phone's Settings, under Apps, then TravelSaathi. This removes everything, including your name and the stops you ticked off.
  • Uninstall the app.

Security

The app talks to my relay server over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and the AI service key is kept on the server, not in the app. I never ask for passwords or payment details. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so I can't guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

TravelSaathi is not intended for children under 13. I don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information through the app, contact me and I'll help you remove it.

Your choices and rights

You control what you type. If you don't want something sent to the AI service, don't type it and avoid including personal details in your messages.

Entering your name is optional. You can skip it and the app works the same way.

Because there is no account, I hold no profile about you and your saved trips can be deleted on your phone as described above. Depending on where you live, for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or the EU's GDPR, you may also have rights to access, correct or erase personal data. To ask about those rights, contact me using the details below.

International data transfers

The services listed above run servers in several countries, so your information may be processed outside the country where you live. By using the app you accept this transfer.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is significant, I'll say so in the app or on the Google Play listing.
